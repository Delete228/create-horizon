# Create 0.9 parity review world

Open `Play.command` in the generated review folder. It starts the
verified local Horizon build in a separate profile and opens
`CreateParityReview09` in Creative. The review profile already uses the
local Create 6.0.10 JAR. Your launcher profiles are separate.

The stands start unpowered so you can inspect them before anything moves.
Yellow tiles mark the drives. The signs give their positions, facing and
speed. Place a Creative Motor and use its speed control, or paste the
commands below into chat to reproduce the fixture. Engineer's Goggles
are already equipped and the Wrench is in slot 1.

| Stand | What to look for |
| --- | --- |
| 01 Water and lava | The three-block water tower shows 15,000 of 24,000 mB. The 3×3×2 lava tank shows 84,000 of 144,000 mB from any part. Try the Wrench on a corner, then restore the windows. |
| 02 Two pumps and a valve | Open the valve, then start both pumps. The left tank starts with 8,000 mB of water. Watch the valve pointer and use goggles on both tanks. The total stays at 8,000 mB and the stone-capped branch stays idle. |
| 02B Active side tank | Start both pumps. The upstream pump at 64 RPM fills the side tank. Break that motor and the downstream pump starts drawing from the side tank toward the right tank. The three tanks conserve 8,000 mB. |
| 02C Water and lava between two pumps | Start both pumps together using the commands below. The junction turns to cobblestone and drops one Fluid Pipe. The right tank receives 90 mB of lava, the side tank keeps 1,910 mB, and the water tank keeps 8,000 mB. |
| 03 Open ends | Start the pump. The full cauldron empties, with 500 mB left at each of the two outlets. To see the reaction, try an unrun copy with a lava source in one outlet. One side makes obsidian and the other places a water source. |
| 04 Gantry | Start the motor and watch the carriage pause at the portable dock. The bottom chest receives three wheat and two marked sticks, then the carriage lands at the east end. |
| 04B Two moving inventories | Start the second Gantry at −1 RPM and open the chest or barrel while it is moving. On landing, check the three marked ingots and the partially worn pickaxe. |
| 05 Piston | Start the motor and watch the vertical piston pause at the dock, unload the same cargo, and continue to its upper landing. |
| 05B Rope Pulley | Lift the chest and barrel together. The chest contains seven diamonds, and the barrel has 23 marked ingots. Stop the motor during travel or let both inventories reach the upper landing. |
| 05C Piston with two inventories | Start the piston at −1 RPM to open either bank while it moves. The chest holds seven marked copper ingots and the barrel a worn iron pickaxe. Both keep their contents at the upper landing. |
| 06 Chute | Start the downward Fan. The seven marked sticks leave the stopped Belt. The Chute shows the airflow and the moving stack. |
| 06B Wet Chute | Put wheat in the top Hopper at (44, 131, 24). Check the bottom Barrel and the two waterlogged windows. |
| 06C Depot suction | Start the downward Fan. Watch the 23 marked sticks cross the air gap and park in the Chute window. The four iron ingots stay in the Depot buffer. |
| 07 Deployer | The hand holds three marked saplings. Start the motor to plant one and leave two in the hand. With the motor removed, remove the sapling and run the drive again. |
| 08 Press | Start the press with eight copper ingots on the Depot. Allow eight strokes. Empty-hand use retrieves the results. |
| 09 Mixer | Start the motor. Look for the water, honey and flour particles while the whisk stirs. Inspect the dough in the Basin after the cycle. |

At stands 04 and 05, open the chest below the stationary Hopper after the
moving interface pulls away. While docked, watch both interface heads extend
toward each other. They retract as the carriage or piston leaves. At stands 04B
and 05C, switch to empty slot 9,
fly level with the cargo in Creative, and right-click either container
while it moves.

To check a settings board after reopening the world, aim at the side panel of
a Creative Motor or Mechanical Piston. Hold right-click to open the board,
move to a new value, and release right-click to apply it. Reopen the board to
read the saved selection.

## Drive commands

```mcfunction
/setblock 26 129 6 create:creative_motor[facing=south]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 20 129 7 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 23 129 7 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:64}
/setblock 4 129 12 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:64}
/setblock 8 129 12 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:16}
/setblock 6 129 24 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 18 129 23 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:-32}
/setblock 18 129 17 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:-1}
/setblock 32 132 23 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:-32}
/setblock 13 135 22 create:creative_motor[facing=west]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 28 132 12 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:-1}
/setblock 39 134 23 create:creative_motor[facing=down]{ScrollValue:-128}
/setblock 46 132 29 create:creative_motor[facing=down]{ScrollValue:-128}
/setblock 35 131 9 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 41 130 9 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:32}
/setblock 43 129 16 create:creative_motor[facing=up]{ScrollValue:32}
```

The first three commands power the valve and the series pumps. The remaining
commands follow the order of the motorized stands, with 02C listed separately
below. Break a motor to stop that drive.

For stand 02C, enter these four commands in order in a fresh copy of the
stand. Freezing the world lets both motors start on the same game tick.

```mcfunction
/tick freeze
/setblock 36 129 5 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:16}
/setblock 40 131 5 create:creative_motor[facing=east]{ScrollValue:64}
/tick unfreeze
```

To compare batch pressing at stand 08, enable `bulkPressing` in the review
profile's `config/create-server.toml` and use a fresh stack on the Depot.
The default is `false`. The setting applies to the next strike, including
on a press that is already installed.

```toml
[recipes]
bulkPressing = true
```

The builder exercises the stands, quits and reopens Horizon during
the Gantry dock, and checks the final inventory and fluid totals. It then
restores the initial stands and checks the delivered save after another
restart. The per-feature Java comparisons and remaining differences live
in [validation](../../VALIDATION.md) and the [roadmap](../roadmap.md).

The [builder](../../tools/parity_review_world.py) takes the verified client
executable, its prepared base resources, `horizon-ctl`, and a local copy
of the pinned Create JAR. The save and screenshots are local artifacts.
